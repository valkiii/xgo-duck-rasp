# XGO-Duck backpack (carrier + cover)

Replaces the Luwu/UNO-size board on the BACK of the battery box with: Feetech FE-URT-2, Pi Zero 2W + PiSugar 3, MPU6050 IMU,
optional rocker switch and DC-jack cradle.  Everything is generated by one parametric script:

    /Users/vc/Research/xgo-duck/.venv/bin/python backpack.py     # ~20 s, rewrites all STL + PNG + report.json

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `carrier.stl` | base plate that screws to the bosses, board standoffs, IMU posts, jack saddles, cover-screw posts (print as exported: flat face down) |
| `cover.stl` | rounded shell, already rotated into print orientation (back face on the bed, open side up) |
| `backpack_assembled_battery_frame.stl` | carrier + cover in battery.stl coordinates (for CAD overlay / fit checks) |
| `electronics_boxes_pack_frame.stl` | the stand-in boxes used for the assembly renders and collision checks (pack frame) |
| `previews/1..8_*.png` | exploded view, cover-off assembly, back view, side view + ghosted battery box, iso on box, print orientations, plan view |
| `report.json` | numbers from the last run (volumes, overlaps, gaps) |

## Layout (and why)

Looking at the robot's back (`previews/8_plan_view_layout.png`):

* **Left column: FE-URT-2**, USB-C end up, terminal block end down, 3-pin servo ports facing the left wall.  It sits on
  8.5 mm standoffs so the **MPU6050 lies flat on the carrier underneath it** (IMU centre = battery x -7.5, z 0.0, i.e. 7.5 mm
  off the centre line, mid-height of the box, 6 mm behind the boss tips, rigid on the carrier, nothing moving near it).
* **Right column: Pi Zero 2W on PiSugar 3 + LiPo**, camera end up, SD end down, micro-USB ports / PiSugar USB-C / button
  facing the right wall (one 60 mm long slot), GPIO header facing the gap.
* **7.4 mm gap** between the columns: the two M3x16 cover screws and the 5 IMU wires (Pi header -> IMU, which sits right under the URT-2 edge).
* **Bay below the URT-2** (bottom end wall = a side wall, not the back): rocker switch cut-out 19x13 and DC-jack hole/cradle (12 mm).
* Wall cut-outs are all open towards the rim, so nothing needs supports: right wall = Pi ports, left wall = servo cables,
  top wall = camera ribbon (Pi column) + USB-C plug (URT column), bottom wall = SD card, rocker, jack.  Vent slots in the roof.

**Why side by side instead of stacked?**  Stacked (URT-2 flat, Pi stack above) needs about 1.6 + 4 + 11.5 + 16 + 2 + 1.6 = 37 mm of depth; side by side
needs **24.8 mm** (limit set by the 16 mm Pi/PiSugar stack + 4.2 mm lift above the screw heads + 3 mm for jumper-connector headroom + 1.6 mm roof).
The price is width: the two boards (30.6 + 37.2 mm pockets + gap) cannot fit in the 54 mm box width, so the pack is **78.4 mm wide** and overhangs the box.

## Overall size, mass

* Pack outer: **78.4 (u, robot x) x 76.0 (v, robot z) x 24.8 mm deep behind the boss tips**.
* In battery.stl coordinates it spans x -37.2..+41.2, z -37.5..+38.5, y -49.4..-24.6 (box is x -26.9..27.0, z -35.0..36.5).
  So it overhangs the box by 10.3 mm (-x) and 14.2 mm (+x) sideways, 2.5 mm below and 2.0 mm above.  Pack centre is shifted +2 mm in x and +0.5 mm in z
  on purpose (boss B2's screw head would otherwise hit the URT-2 corner post; boss B1's head must clear the Pi stack end).
* Back surface: flat back with 8 mm radius edge rounding limited to 45 deg, plan corner radius 5.5 mm.  Total cover wall/roof 1.6 mm, plate 1.6 mm.
* Solid volume / solid-PLA mass (1.24 g/cm3): carrier **10.12 cm3 = 12.6 g**, cover **14.07 cm3 = 17.4 g**, total **30.0 g**.  A slicer with 4 perimeters and
  15 % infill will give roughly 70-80 % of that (my estimate, ~21-24 g).  Electronics not included.
* All meshes watertight/manifold (trimesh `is_watertight` True for both STLs after re-loading).
* Fit check against `battery.stl` in the same frame: intersection volume carrier 0.000 mm3, cover 0.000 mm3; the carrier just touches the boss/rail tips (gap 0),
  the cover rim stays 0.76 mm off the box.  No overlap between cover, carrier and any electronics stand-in (script prints the table).

## What I measured from battery.stl (corrections to the brief)

* Boss tips y = -24.6 (also the tips of the two side rails at |x| 21.9..26.2, z -7..13 and the upper corner blocks) - the carrier rests flat on all of them.  Back wall of the box y = -20.6..-18.5.
* Boss centres (x, z): B1 (19.1, 33.2), B2 (-8.9, 33.2), B3 (-24.1, -17.6), B4 (24.1, -18.9); OD 5.5 (B2: 6.0).
* **Pilot holes: only THREE bosses have one, and they have different depths from the tip: B1 3.9 mm, B2 5.9 mm, B3 7.9 mm.  B4 is a solid pin (no hole).**
  So "7.8 mm everywhere" is not true; with B1 only 3.9 mm deep the thread engagement there is limited by the box, not by the carrier.

## Screws

**Carrier -> box bosses.  M3x16 are too long for this** (the screws sit under the boards, so the heads must be only 1.4 mm above the plate; a 16 mm screw would go ~14 mm into the box and hit the cell).
Buy short round/pan-head self-tapping-style M3: **B1: M3x5 (3.6 mm engagement), B2: M3x6 (4.6 mm), B3: M3x8 (6.6 mm)**.  Simplest: 3x M3x5 everywhere (3.6 mm each).
B4 has no hole: the plate simply rests on it.  If you want a 4th screw, drill the pin with a 2.8 mm bit about 6 mm deep, set `use_b4_screw=True` in `build_carrier()` and use M3x6.

| use | screw | count | engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| carrier to boss B1 / B2 / B3 | M3x5 / M3x6 / M3x8 (round head, head dia <= 5.8, height <= 2.4) | 1 / 1 / 1 | 3.6 / 4.6 / 6.6 mm |
| FE-URT-2 to carrier | M3x8 round head into 2.8 mm pilot | 4 | 6.4 mm |
| Pi + PiSugar stack to carrier | M2.5x12 into 2.2 mm pilot | 4 | 6.4 mm (check, see below) |
| IMU to carrier | M2.5x6 into 2.2 mm pilot | 2 | 4.4 mm |
| cover to carrier (gap posts) | **M3x16 (yours)** into 2.8 mm pilot | 2 | 8 mm |

The Pi screw length assumes the Pi PCB top is 5.6 mm above the post tops (1.6 PCB + 2.4 PiSugar-Pi spacing + 1.6 PiSugar PCB); measure your stack and use (that distance + 6.4) mm.
The cover also has 4 snap bumps (top/bottom end walls) that click into grooves in the plate edge, and it wraps the plate with 0.25 mm slip fit.

## ASSUMED dimensions - please measure (all are named constants at the top of `backpack.py`)

| constant | assumed | how to measure |
|---|---|---|
| `URT_L`, `URT_W` | 55.8 x 36.6 mm PCB | calipers, PCB only |
| `URT_HOLE_D`, `URT_HOLE_INSET` | 3.2 mm holes, 3.5 mm from edges | calipers on the 4 holes (edge to hole edge + radius); the standoff pattern follows this |
| `URT_TOTAL_H` | 11.5 mm | lay the board flat, measure from the table to the top of the tallest part (blue terminal / 5-pin header) - roof clearance is ~1.2 mm only |
| connector positions (in `electronics()`) | terminal at the short edge, 3-pin ports on one long edge, USB-C on the opposite short edge, 4-pin on the other long edge | only matter for the wall cut-out positions: servo slot `SERVO_NOTCH_V`, USB-C notch (top wall, centred on the board), width 14 mm |
| `PI_STACK_H` | 16.0 mm | stack on a table, bottom of the LiPo to the top of the highest Pi part (header or chip) |
| `LIPO_T`, `LIPO_L/W` | 6.0 mm thick, 50 x 24 mm | LiPo thickness = distance LiPo underside -> PiSugar PCB underside; footprint must stay clear of the 4 corner posts (drives post height 10.2 mm) |
| PiSugar USB-C / button position | unknown | look where they sit along the long edge with the Pi's micro-USB ports; the right-wall slot is 59.6 mm long x 19 mm high, so they should be covered - shorten with `PI_BRD[2] + 3.0` if not wanted |
| Pi hole pattern | 58 x 23 mm, 3.5 inset (datasheet) | check the PiSugar has the same 4 holes (some PiSugar versions only have 2) |
| `IMU_L/W`, `IMU_HOLE_D`, `IMU_HOLE_SPAN` | 21 x 16 mm, 2 holes dia 3.0, 15.5 mm apart on the long axis | calipers.  **The IMU header must not stand up** (only ~2 mm free under the URT-2): solder the 5 wires straight to the pads, or use a right-angle/low header pointing sideways to the gap |
| `ROCKER_CUT_W/H`, `ROCKER_BODY_DEPTH` | 19 x 13 mm panel hole, 15 mm body | calipers; body must fit in the 16 mm bay below the URT-2 |
| `JACK_D`, `JACK_LEN_IN` | 12 mm, 14 mm | diameter of the green barrel-jack adapter body; saddles are 2.4 mm wide with zip-tie room |
| `M3_HEAD_D/H` | 5.8 x 2.4 mm | measure your M3 round heads; if the head is higher than 2.4 mm the Pi stack (4.2 mm above plate) may touch it |
| `GAP`, header headroom `HEADER_CLEAR` | 7.4 mm, 3.0 mm | dupont plugs pushed on the Pi header need ~8 mm of height: use right-angle sockets or solder wires, otherwise raise `HEADER_CLEAR` (depth grows 1:1) |

## Print settings

* PLA or (better, the robot falls) **PETG**; 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, **no supports, no brim needed**.
* Carrier: as exported, flat face down; 3-4 perimeters, 20-25 % infill, 4 top/bottom layers (the plate is only 1.6 mm).
* Cover: as exported (back face on the bed).  4 perimeters (1.6 mm wall = 4 lines), 3 top/bottom layers, 15 % infill.  Roof-down printing gives a smooth back surface;
  the edge rounding never exceeds 45 deg.  Bridges: rocker hole 19 mm, jack hole 12.6 mm, screw counterbores 6 mm - use full part-cooling fan.
* Keep elephant-foot compensation off on the cover (the roof is the visible surface).  All board pockets carry 0.3 mm clearance per side, plate-to-cover fit is 0.25 mm.
* Pilot holes are modelled at the right size for self-tapping into plastic (M3 2.8 mm, M2.5 2.2 mm); do not over-tighten.

## Assembly order

1. Print, then screw the carrier to the box (3 short M3 screws; B4 rests).  2. Mount the IMU (2x M2.5x6) and solder/plug its 5 wires.
3. Fit URT-2 on its 4 standoffs (4x M3x8).  4. Mount the Pi/PiSugar stack (4x M2.5x12).  5. Rocker + jack (optional, jack with a zip-tie).
6. Slide the cover on until the snap bumps click, add 2x M3x16 through the roof counterbores.

## Open points / risks (not verifiable from the CAD)

* **Side clearance**: the pack is 78.4 mm wide (box 54 mm).  I could not check it against the thighs/hips because those STLs are in other frames.  Check on the real robot that the left/right
  overhang (10-14 mm) and the 2-2.5 mm top/bottom overhang do not touch the thigh shells, hip servos or neck servo while walking and falling.  If it does, the fallback is the stacked layout (~56 x 72 x 37 mm, not generated).
* Cable from Pi micro-USB (right wall) to URT-2 USB-C (top wall) has to run outside around the top end: use a short right-angle cable; the gutter above the Pi stack is 7.2 mm.
* The three box bosses are only 3.9 / 5.9 / 7.9 mm deep: the original design was equally weak.  If the plate loosens, glue a M3 brass insert or tap the bosses deeper.
* Everything above the plate is a stand-in box model; real connector heights/positions may differ.
