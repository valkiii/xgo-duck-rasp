# battery_urt2.stl - XGO-Duck battery box with FE-URT-2 + MPU6050 mount

Drop-in replacement for `structure/battery.stl` (same coordinate frame, same outer
dimensions, flanges, battery cavity). Only the BACK face (toward -y) changed.
Everything is generated by `modify_battery.py` (named constants at the top; re-run with
`/Users/vc/Research/xgo-duck/.venv/bin/python modify_battery.py`).

Files: `battery_urt2.stl`, `preview_back.png`, `preview_iso.png`, `preview_side.png`,
`report.json` (numbers below), `standin_urt2.stl` / `standin_imu_envelope.stl` (collision stand-ins).

## What the original looks like (measured by slicing the mesh)
- Box x -26.9..27.0, z -35.0..36.5, y -24.6..4.4 (y -24.6 = old UNO boss tips).
- Back plate: outer surface y = -20.6, inner surface y = -18.6 (2.0 mm), with the two bow-tie
  windows (z -9..12) and 4 side slots. Two shallow recesses (39 x 4 mm, 1 mm deep, z 23.7..27.7
  and z -23.1..-19.1) are cut into the outer face - the new standoffs sit on/over them and fill
  them locally.
- UNO bosses stand 4 mm off the plate (to y = -24.6) at the 4 UNO positions; side rails (|x| > 21)
  and top corner brackets also reach y = -24.6.

## What changed
1. 4 new standoffs (OD 6.0, 7.0 mm above the plate outer face) for the FE-URT-2 at
   x = +-14.6, z = 25.35 / -23.85 (49.2 x 29.2 rectangle, centred x = 0, z = 0.75 = centre of the
   box z-range). Pilot holes: Ø2.5 mm, 6.0 mm deep (M3 self-tapping).
2. 2 short bosses (OD 5.5, 2.0 mm above the plate) for the MPU6050 at x = +-8.25, z = 14.0.
   Pilot holes: Ø2.2 mm, 3.0 mm deep (2.0 boss + 1.0 into the plate; 1.0 mm of plate is left, the
   cavity is not pierced).
3. Old UNO bosses/rails/brackets left untouched: nothing of them is under the new boards
   (they are at |x| >= 16 near z = 33 or at |x| >= 21, and only reach y = -24.6, 3 mm below the
   URT-2 underside at y = -27.6).
4. Nothing else changed: material removed from the original = the two IMU pilot holes only
   (7.6 mm3, y -20.6..-19.6); the cavity side (y > -18.6) is identical.
All new features lie within |x| <= 17.6 (limit 26.9) and z -26.85..28.35 (box -35..36.5).

## Depth behind the back face
| surface | y | behind plate outer face (y=-20.6) | behind old boss tips (y=-24.6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| standoff tops / URT-2 underside | -27.6 | 7.0 mm | 3.0 mm |
| **URT-2 PCB top (1.6 mm PCB assumed)** | **-29.2** | **8.6 mm** | **4.6 mm** |
Parts on top of the URT-2 (terminal block, connectors, USB-C) add to that; they are not modelled.
The IMU stack (boss 2.0 + 4.5 max) tops out at 6.5 mm, leaving 0.5 mm below the URT-2 underside.
If you want more air there raise `URT_STANDOFF_H` (script re-checks the clearance).

## Orientation of the boards (view from the back, looking along +y: +x to the right, +z up)
- FE-URT-2: long side (56.2) vertical, x -18.25..18.25, z -27.35..28.85. Screw terminal on the TOP
  short edge (+z), USB-C on the BOTTOM edge (-z). The hole pattern is symmetric, so it also fits
  rotated 180 deg (terminal at the bottom) - the left/right side of the two 3-pin servo connectors
  follows from how your board is laid out; both long edges (x = +-18.25) hang over nothing but the
  plate: the old side rails/brackets only reach y = -24.6, i.e. 3 mm below the PCB underside.
- MPU6050: lies flat, component side facing OUT (-y, toward the URT-2), long axis vertical,
  x -11..11, z -34..16.5. Mounting-hole edge at the TOP (+z, holes at z = 14), header edge at the
  BOTTOM: pins from z = -13 to -20, Dupont housings from z = -20 to -34, so the jumper ends point
  down (-z) and the wires leave through the open notch at the bottom of the back plate
  (|x| < 10, z < -27) / past the box bottom (z = -35), under the URT-2 edge. With the header down
  and the silkscreen readable from the back, the board's right = +x, its up (header -> holes) = +z
  and its Z (out of the component face) = -y of the STL frame. (If the X/Y arrows on your board
  are printed along the board's long axis/width, that is the mapping to use: board "up" = +z.)
  (Rotating the 51 mm IMU 90 deg to exit sideways does not fit inside the box width.)

## Screws
- URT-2: 4x M3x6 self-tapping/thread-forming (4.4 mm engaged after the 1.6 mm PCB). For M2.5x6
  set `URT_PILOT_D = 2.2` and rerun.
- IMU: 2x M2.5x4 (2.4 mm engaged; pilot is 3.0 mm deep). M2.5x5 would bottom out (3.4 > 3.0 mm) -
  do not use unless you accept piercing the plate.
  Screw heads (~1.5 mm) stay below the 6.5 mm stack.

## Printing
Same orientation as the original: I assumed open (+y) side down, back plate on top (the box is
open toward +y), so the standoffs grow straight up from the plate with vertical pilot holes:
no supports and no new overhangs (all six footprints are fully on solid plate; verified,
0.00 mm2 overhang). Standoffs partly straddle the two 1 mm recesses and the old top-right bracket;
they simply fuse with them. If you print back-face down instead, the standoffs are the first
layers and need nothing either, but the plate then bridges as in the original.
Pilot holes may need a 0.1-0.2 mm tweak for your printer (`URT_PILOT_D`, `IMU_PILOT_D`).
